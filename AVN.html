CYBERSPACE—Ultra Flesh Archives presents Fierce: The Rhonda Jo Petty Collection, the latest limited edition release in an ongoing series of classic 8mm and Super 8mm films from the 1970s and early 1980s.

“Often billed as the 'Farrah Fawcett' of erotica, Petty had an appeal all her own. Her undeniable on-screen presence made her a legend of the Golden Age of porn,” a video description reads.

The special edition Blu-ray release compiles 15 of Petty’s 8mm and Super 8mm films, all transferred and restored in high-definition, including full commentary with Petty herself discussing her life and career.

The new collectible release is limited to 750 individually numbered units and also features:

  • Authentic film projector sound
  • Bonus Rhonda Jo Petty film
  • Four-panel color insert
  • Photo gallery
  • Restoration featurette
  • Rare “Disco Sex Action” promo record
  • Double-sided cover art
  • Trailers
  • Die cut info card including an 8mm film strip from one of Rhonda Jo Petty’s films
  • Rhonda Jo Petty trading card housed in a spot gloss side load sleeve
  • Special “Signature Edition” limited to the first 50 numbered copies

“Ultra Flesh Archives is dedicated to the preservation and restoration of 8mm and Super 8mm films from ‘the Golden Age of Porn.’ UFA’s first four releases, Wildheart: The Leslie Winston Collection, Effervescent: The Serena Collection Vol. 1 and Endless EXXXstasy Volumes 1 and 2, have set at new standard in quality with their presentation of classic small format erotic films with many more releases planned for 2027,” a company representative said.

For more information or to purchase Fierce: The Rhonda Jo Petty Collection, visit UltraFleshArchives.com.
