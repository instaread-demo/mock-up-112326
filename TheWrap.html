How Tech Investing, Not Acting, May Land Ashton Kutcher in the Billionaire’s Club

Pro Available to WrapPRO members

Anthropic’s IPO is the Hollywood actor’s latest windfall, capping off a string of successful bets including Uber, Spotify and OpenAI

Ashton Kutcher already made a name for himself as a Hollywood actor who had a knack for picking the right tech startups to invest in. But his latest bet on Anthropic, which went public in a record-breaking IPO, could finally get him into the billionaire’s club.

Anthropic shares closed at $97.17 after its first day of trading, valuing the company at $1.76 trillion. Kutcher invested in the AI powerhouse through Sound Ventures, which owns a 0.5% stake now worth more than an estimated $7 billion. 

“People usually see the one deal and think that’s the whole thing, but the ones who know realize that it’s years of work, micro victories and failures stacked on top of one another. It’s really the manifestation of trust. The folks that trusted us, our vision and our thesis, and leaders of companies that trusted that we would bring more than money. Kind of cool when that works out. Congrats to @DarioAmodei and the whole team at Anthropic,” Kutcher wrote in a post on X. “Congrats to the LPs that supported us. Congrats to our team that helped us craft and execute a vision. And thank you to those who didn’t believe in us. You made us work harder.”
