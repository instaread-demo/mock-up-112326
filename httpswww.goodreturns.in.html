      Gold rates and silver rates in India witnessed buying momentum on Monday, after a week-long mega crash due to fresh buying in the global bullion market. MCX gold surged closer to Rs 1.25 lakh mark with nearly 2.5% upside, while MCX silver futures crossed Rs 1.51 lakh mark before mildy correcting. Both precious metals are up by 2-3%.

      Spot gold jumps 2% to trade above $3,610 per ounce, and spot silver gains 3% to trade near $42.50 per ounce.

      This is amidst a decline in crude oil prices, with US WTI falling marginally to trade above $83 per barrel and Brent crude declining 0.5% to trade around $92 per barrel.

      Despite the uptick in bullion, spot gold and silver are still at their lowest level since September 2025, and crude oil prices remain elevated as well. This week, major focus will be on US PCE inflation data.

      Also, the US dollar mildly pulled back from the 104 mark to trade around 103.76 on November 23, 2026. Notably, the dollar has gained nearly 2% over the past month and nearly 4% over the last 12 months, becoming the major reason for sharp declines in gold and silver.

      The probability of a rate hike in the December 2026 policy by the US Federal Reserve has shrunk the appetite for gold. The escalating geopolitical crisis has kept inflationary pressures on the roll, and several FOMC policymakers indicate that additional rate hikes cannot be ignored.

      Oil shipments continued to move through the Persian Gulf despite a recent increase in sporadic clashes, including attacks on tankers navigating the Strait of Hormuz. Last week, the IEA forecast a global supply surplus of around 2 million barrels per day this quarter, while Goldman Sachs cautioned that an oversupply could emerge next year. Meanwhile, Iran launched attacks on Bahrain over the weekend as the Islamic Republic continued targeting US assets in neighboring countries. Despite these developments, President Donald Trump has so far refrained from launching further strikes against Iran following the midterm elections. However, he indicated over the weekend that he may consider resuming military action after the New Year if diplomatic negotiations fail to produce an agreement, as per Trading Economics.

      Live Gold Rate: Gold Rates In Mumbai Today

      On November 23, the 24-carra or 999 purity gold price rallied by Rs 14,800 in 100 grams to Rs 12,65,600 and surged Rs 1,480 to Rs 1,26,560 per 10 grams in Mumbai. In the same carat, 8 grams gold zoomed Rs 1,184 to Rs 1,01,248 and 1 gram is up by Rs 148 to Rs 12,656.
      Furthermore, 22-carat gold is available at Rs 11.60 lakh per 100 grams, higher by Rs 13,500 on Monday. In the 916 purity, 10 grams gold surged Rs 1,350 to Rs 1,16,000 and 8 grams gold jumped Rs 1,080 to Rs 92,800. Lastly, 1 gram gold price is up by Rs 135 to Rs 11,600.
      In the 750 purity or 18-carat, gold price jumped Rs 11,000 to Rs 9,49,100 per 100 grams, soared Rs 1,100 to Rs 94,910 per 10 grams, rose Rs 880 to Rs 75,928 per 8 grams and is up Rs 110 to Rs 9,491 per 1 gram.

      Gold rates in Mumbai climbed sharply on Monday, after recording their steepest decline in 8 months last week. Gold, which was near 15-month lows, has rebounded to hit above Rs 1.26 lakh per 10 grams. The latest uptrend in gold and silver can be attributed to the decline in crude oil prices and US dollar and treasury yields. However, the expectation of a rate hike in the December 2026 policy continues to loom and keep the outlook for precious metals volatile.

      Live Gold Rate: Gold Prices Surge Sharply After 14-Month Low, Are Bulls Returning?

      Gold's rebound follows its steepest weekly decline in eight months, as a technology-led sell-off in global equity markets prompted a shift towards traditional safe-haven assets. The decline in US Treasury yields and the dollar provided additional support, while comments from Treasury Secretary Bessent warning that excessive monetary tightening could hurt economic growth prompted traders to scale back expectations of near-term Federal Reserve rate hikes. Meanwhile, central banks purchased 321 tonnes of gold in the third quarter, reinforcing the underlying physical demand and providing a cushion against deeper corrections. Despite the sharp rebound, the technical outlook remains cautious. A decisive reclaim and sustained move above $3,630 would strengthen the recovery and improve prospects of an extension towards $3,700–3,730. Conversely, failure to clear the immediate resistance band could leave prices vulnerable to renewed selling, with a break below $3,500 exposing the next support near $3,380: Ponmudi R, CEO of Enrich Money.
